Source recording: Horror scream1 — Vinrax

SkeletonHurt01..03 are derived from this recording:
separate crops, pitch/rate variation, high/low-pass filtering, a short baked
spectral echo, fades and peak normalization. Mono PCM, 22050 Hz.
The derived mixes are also dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.
No film recordings are used. The earlier ghost-moan/bone mixes were replaced.
See docs/ENEMY-FEEDBACK.md for segment mapping and playback settings.

Attack source recording: Ghostmonster sounds — iletzy
https://opengameart.org/content/ghostmonster-sounds
Recording: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Ghostmonster%20sounds.mp3
License: CC0 1.0 (same dedication as above).
SkeletonAttack01..03 use separate low growl/breath excerpts, down-pitched
(rate 0.74–0.88), filtered 100–4800 Hz, with a short baked 29 ms echo and fades.
These replace the higher scream-derived attack clips; pain clips are unchanged.

SkeletonDeath01..03 also derive from Vinrax's CC0 Horror scream1 recording:
short excerpts, rate changes, 160–6500 Hz filtering, onset/release fades and
level normalization. Final lengths: 0.70/0.80/0.85 seconds. CC0 1.0.
See docs/COMBAT-FOLLOWUP.md for source start times and playback settings.
